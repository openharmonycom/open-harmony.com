Smart monitoring for power distribution areas

A connected monitoring solution built on OpenHarmony distributed soft bus technology, helping power teams bring devices together and respond closer to the edge.

⌁▣◉⌂⌘
Home / Solutions / Smart distribution monitoring

Smart distribution gateways use OpenHarmony's distributed soft bus to discover and connect sensors and equipment in a power distribution room. They create edge computing capabilities for faster communication and safer systems. The connected devices can coordinate locally without requiring a dedicated IoT model for each scenario.

What the solution enables

⌁

Plug and play

Devices can discover one another and form a network automatically, reducing manual setup when equipment is added.

◉

Coordinated monitoring

Video and security equipment can work together, while pumps and ventilation fans respond as part of a coordinated system.

⌂

Simpler maintenance

Staff can connect a handheld device directly to the edge gateway and retrieve logs with a tap—no laptop required.

How it works

01 · ConnectSensors and equipment join the local network.
02 · UnderstandThe gateway gathers device signals and context.
03 · RespondEdge logic coordinates monitoring and equipment.
04 · MaintainTechnicians retrieve logs from a handheld device.
By combining automatic device networking with local edge capabilities, the solution supports faster decisions and reduces the need for people to be present for routine monitoring and maintenance.
