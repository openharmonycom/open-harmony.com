Explore a connected phone experience

Tap around a small, browser-based OpenHarmony-inspired interface. Open sample apps, adjust quick settings, and return to the home screen at any time.

A browser demo

Try the interface

This lightweight demo shows how a phone experience can connect apps and system controls in one place. It runs locally in your browser and does not install software or access your device.

  • Tap an app icon to open its sample screen.
  • Tap the status bar or swipe down to open quick settings.
  • Use the Home button or swipe up to return.

This is an interface prototype, not a HarmonyOS system emulator. It cannot run native apps or reproduce real device services.
